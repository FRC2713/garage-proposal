# FRC Practice Field Research — Handoff Brief

**Prepared:** October 7, 2026
**For:** Melrose Red Hawk Robotics Educational Foundation (501(c)(3) supporting FRC Team 2713, Melrose High School, Melrose, MA)
**Goal:** Build a proposal for a full-size FRC practice field at Melrose High School.
**Published report page:** https://claude.ai/artifact/F35tE95DbDEYrvjctJ9vxd

## Research questions

1. How many successful teams have access to half- or full-size fields within their schools?
2. What is the typical square footage teams have in similarly sized schools?
3. What is the typical budget of a successful FRC team?

## Key caveat

No public dataset answers Q1 or Q2. FIRST does not track team facilities, and no source compares team space by school enrollment. The findings below are from published examples, not a representative sample. A survey is planned to fill this gap (see the last section).

---

## Q2: Square footage

**Finding:** A full field needs about 2,800 sq ft and a working shop about 3,000 sq ft. Single-school facilities at top programs run 6,500–7,800 sq ft. Space needs are driven by the field size, which is the same regardless of school size.

| Space                                                  | Approx. sq ft | Basis                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Typical classroom (reference only)                     | ~900          | General rule of thumb, not sourced                    |
| Half field with margins                                | ~1,000–1,400  | ~27 × 27 ft carpet plus margins                       |
| Full field carpet only                                 | ~1,458        | 27 × 54 ft (exact dimensions vary slightly by season) |
| Full field with room for driver stations and equipment | ~2,800        | ~40 × 70 ft, per Chief Delphi                         |
| Shop space target                                      | ~3,000        | One team's planned shop, per Chief Delphi             |
| 180 S.P.A.M. facility                                  | 6,500         | Team website                                          |
| 1678 Citrus Circuits facility                          | 7,800         | CA Dept. of Education                                 |
| Fremont Union HSD center (5 schools)                   | 14,500        | News coverage                                         |

Notes:

- Mentors generally treat a little over 50% of a field as the practical minimum. A half field doesn't work for games where scoring areas are at opposite ends.
- FRC carpet comes in 15 ft rolls; extra width on each side of the 27 ft field is needed to secure the border.

Sources: https://www.chiefdelphi.com/t/full-field-practice-facilities-how/128477 ; https://www.chiefdelphi.com/t/what-is-the-smallest-possible-practice-field/469654 ; https://www.chiefdelphi.com/t/whats-your-ideal-robot-workspace/119771

## Q3: Annual budgets

**Finding:** Competitive district teams typically spend $50,000–80,000/year; world-level programs spend $150,000+. Most spending goes to registration and travel, not the robot.

### Suggested tiers

| Tier                                      | Annual budget   |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| Functional                                | ~$15,000–30,000 |
| Competitive district team, reaches Worlds | ~$50,000–80,000 |
| Perennial world-level program             | $150,000+       |

## Implications for Melrose

- 2713 already fundraises at a competitive-district level ($80k+ record season). Compared with peers, the gap is facilities, not funding.
- The closest models (Davis, Madera, Martin County) each got a dedicated field via district facilities funded by bonds or grants. Massachusetts equivalents (e.g., MSBA projects, CTE grants) were not researched.
- An in-school field avoids rent (some teams pay ~$30k/yr), reduces travel to shared fields like WPI's, and gives the district a venue for scrimmages and younger FIRST programs.

## Planned survey (to be built manually in Google Forms)

**Title:** U.S. FRC Team Facilities & Budget Survey

**Description:** Team 2713 Red Hawk Robotics (Melrose, MA) is gathering data on practice space, team size, and budgets from FRC teams across the U.S. to support a practice-field proposal. It takes about 2 minutes, and estimates are fine. We'll share the summarized results with every team that responds; individual answers won't be published.

All questions required. Responses linked to a Google Sheet.

1. **What is your FRC team number?** — Short answer; number validation 1–99999. (Used to look up team performance on The Blue Alliance.)
2. **What state is your team based in?** — Dropdown: 50 states alphabetically, then District of Columbia, Puerto Rico, Other U.S. territory, Outside the U.S.
3. **About how many students have been on your team, on average, over the last 4 years?** — Multiple choice. Help text: "Count students who were regularly active during build season. If your team is newer than 4 years, average the seasons you've had." Options: Under 15; 15 to 25; 26 to 40; 41 to 60; Over 60.
4. **Where does your team usually drive on a practice field?** — Multiple choice. Help text: "Pick the option you use most during build season." Options: Full-size field inside our school building; Half or partial field inside our school building; Full or partial field at an off-site space our team controls (sponsor, rented, or nonprofit-owned); Shared field run by another team, college, or regional group; No regular practice field (classroom, hallway, parking lot, etc.).
5. **About how much floor space does your team have for its shop and practice area combined?** — Multiple choice. Help text: "For reference, a typical classroom is roughly 900 sq ft, and a full FRC field with room around it is about 2,800 sq ft." Options: Under 1,000 sq ft; 1,000 to 2,500 sq ft; 2,500 to 5,000 sq ft; 5,000 to 8,000 sq ft; Over 8,000 sq ft; Not sure.
6. **What is your approximate total annual budget, including registration, travel, and robot costs?** — Multiple choice. Options: Under $20,000; $20,000 to $40,000; $40,000 to $80,000; $80,000 to $150,000; Over $150,000; Prefer not to say.
7. **About how many students attend the high school your team is based at?** — Multiple choice. Help text: "If your team draws from several schools or is community-based, choose the last option." Options: Under 500; 500 to 1,000; 1,000 to 1,500; 1,500 to 2,500; Over 2,500; Multi-school or community team.

**Confirmation message:** "Thanks! We'll share the summarized results with all responding teams."

**Analysis ideas:** Join responses to The Blue Alliance data by team number to segment by performance (e.g., Worlds/Einstein qualifiers vs. others). Compute sq ft per student and budget per student using team-size midpoints. Compare in-school field access rates by enrollment band and by state/district.
